PREPARATION GUIDE

Improve Chinese proficiency through weekly action.

This is an independent preparation guide for students considering the SJTU Pre-University context—not an admissions guarantee.

The official 2026 program page describes a non-degree pathway intended to strengthen language skills, academic performance and cultural adaptability. It lists HSK 4 ≥180 or equivalent for the Chinese Program. Requirements can change; verify them on the official site.

12-WEEK STARTER PATH

Turn a broad goal into a repeatable routine.

Start small. Consistency is more useful than an unrealistic schedule that collapses after one week.

听

Weeks 1–4 · Listening

Build daily listening habits around classroom instructions, campus directions and introductions.

说

Weeks 5–8 · Speaking

Use short voice diaries, language partners and simple explanations of school subjects.

学

Weeks 9–12 · Academic use

Read short educational texts, review Mathematics and science terms and write structured summaries.
