HONEST RETROSPECTIVE
====================
THIS MADE IT WORSE — Version 1’s member popups became visually elaborate while exposing more personal information and distracting from classes and projects. The final people page is simpler and removes public social handles.

THIS DIDN’T HELP THE OUTPUT YET — Version 2 clarified the audience, but it mostly produced notes. The actual source code, navigation, branding and broken interactions remained unchanged until later versions.

THIS MADE IT WORSE — Version 3’s “交” mark and SJTU-like red branding looked more polished but increased the chance that the student website could be mistaken for an official university property. The final output reverses that decision completely.

THIS DIDN’T FULLY HELP — Version 4 fixed the logo problem but still delivered a one-page prototype with abstract content and little real documentation.

THIS DIDN’T HELP — Version 5 added Chinese preparation, applications and AI news to one homepage. The scope was useful, but the page became longer and the coding identity became less clear. It still did not document real club life. The final output splits those topics into separate pages and grounds them in the report and source folders.

THIS DIDN’T HELP — The old news popup created the appearance of an article reader but did not load article content. It made the experience less trustworthy. The final output uses direct, transparent source links.

Montserrat alone also did not help Chinese readability enough. The final output pairs it with Noto Sans SC.
