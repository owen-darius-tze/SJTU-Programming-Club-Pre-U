1. CREATE A NEW FOCUSED PAGE
----------------------------

3. BILINGUAL TEXT PATTERN FROM VERSION 1, CLEANED UP
----------------------------------------------------
<h2
  data-en="Project-based learning"
  data-zh="项目式学习">
  Project-based learning
</h2>

<p
  data-en="Students learn by building and reflecting."
  data-zh="学生通过实践与反思进行学习。">
  Students learn by building and reflecting.
</p>

Do not place commas between HTML attributes. Version 1 contained examples such as:
  data-en="...", data-zh="..."
The comma makes the markup invalid. Use spaces only.
