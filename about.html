WHO WE ARE · 关于我们

Three students envisioned more than just a club.

Owen, Anqi and Elson—three passionate students from three different countries—dreamed of a dynamic place where coding enthusiasts, from curious beginners to aspiring developers, could connect, collaborate and grow together.

02

OUR VISION · 我们的愿景

Projects, friendships and skills beyond the classroom.

Our vision is to create an unforgettable, interactive and collaborative experience: a community that leaves a lasting mark on every member’s Pre-University journey and builds a legacy that continues beyond one academic year.

03

OUR MISSION · 我们的使命

Cooperate, create, build and share.

Through cooperation, creativity and hands-on building, we create engaging projects, tackle real-world problems and share our passion for technology. Every member should be able to contribute, learn and lead in an inclusive environment.
